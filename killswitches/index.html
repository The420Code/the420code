What a kill switch is

A kill switch is not a caveat and not a disclaimer. It names the specific finding that would end a claim, and where that finding would have to be looked for. If the thing named is found, the claim does not get amended. It dies, and the death is shown.

Six of the six hundred and two are non-negotiable: they are constitutive of the architecture, and firing one ends the work rather than amending it. One is global — KS-V.1, the operational invariance of the Actualization State. If it fires, everything on this site dies with it.
