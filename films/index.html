Ø Films

Eight chains, one hundred and forty films, for the people who would rather watch than read.

These are the films. Most are short, one claim each. A chapter’s film carries the chapter’s whole argument, in four to eight minutes. The drawing draws itself. The films add G’s voice and the drawing, not a new claim.

Nothing plays until you open it. Every film sits in a closed row; open the row and the film loads, leave it closed and the page fetches nothing at all. Every film’s lines are in the page as text, in order, so a chain reads with nothing playing.

The films are served from media.the420code.org. There is no third-party player: the page plays the file.

Want a book? Start at the heart and the philosophy. Want a chapter? Every chapter of Ø Dissolutions, Ø Resolutions, Ø Horizons and Ø Applications has its film. Want the argument? Start at the axiom and the front door. Want the physics? Start at why 21 is forced.
