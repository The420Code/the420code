## The note, for the record

> 18 September 2026. One matter of record, on the author's ruling of this day. It does not alter
> the body above.
>
> 4. §9: the standing of the one interior. §9 carries the one interior as “the 420 Code’s
> strongest structural claim, held as a claim”, its formal topological derivation owed at
> D-ID.1. On the author’s ruling of 18 September 2026 it stands as the floor, not as a claim
> held: one reality, one now, one interior, one awareness — performed in the act, as the axiom
> is. One reality is performed because a record made at one site is read at another, and two
> realities could not do that. One now is performed because every reading, every question and
> every attempt to find a second now is made now; no site reads any now, its own included
> (KS-GLASS.2, AP51; AP52). One awareness is the code’s name for those two together with the one
> rate — the operators numerically many, the awareness they execute one (AP51 §2). The forcing
> of the ethic in §9.2 is unchanged; it now runs through a floor rather than a held premise.
> KS-ID.1 is unchanged: it fires on a second neck, a sequence of writing not reducible to the
> one. Its neighbours are unchanged: the rate at KS-GLASS.3, carrying P-GLASS.1, and the
> reader’s configuration at KS-GLASS.1. One description is withdrawn from the master book, where
> it stood as the measurement under this claim — every window has to read the same now; two
> honest readings of that now, disagreeing beyond what the instruments allow. It was never the
> registered condition and cannot be run, because no site reads any now. D-ID.1 is unchanged:
> PAID at the structural register (AP51 §2), the topological formalisation remaining — a floor
> can be formally stated, as the axiom’s is at AP20; the debt is the statement, not the standing.
> Registered at MKSR v5.33 (18 September 2026). AP43-D1 and AP43-D4 are untouched.
