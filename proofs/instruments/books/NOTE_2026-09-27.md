# The Five Doors, airtight — 27 September 2026

G, 27 September 2026: "please make every and each error — there is no waiting for it to do later —
everything must be fixed — being after religion and the other 3 doors are now the absolute priority —
there can be no scholarly slips, and everything should be airtight."

Every page of the five books online was read line by line and every outside fact in them checked
against its source; the three long originals were read the same way; every correction was read again
by readers who had not written it. What came out is 315 changes to the pages and 248 to the three
original books, each with the reason beside it, and three lines held for the author, on which he said
yes the same evening: *over* thirteen billion years, *where the sacred was first found*, and, after
Rachel's own cry at Mainz, *The soldiers showed none.*

The pages took them in the text they are built from. The books take them here. Each was rebuilt from a
COPY of its Word source of record, hashed before and after and never opened for writing, every edit
asserted against the exact words it replaces, and exported through Word (`WC/tools/brief6_books_0927.py`).
The short editions are set from the library's own text, as on 25 September (`short_editions_0925.py`),
which now carries the italics the books print.
