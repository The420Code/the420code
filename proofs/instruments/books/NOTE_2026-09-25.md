# The seven short editions rebuilt, and The Interior at 2.0.7 — 25 September 2026

On 24 September the pages took the author's corrections and the eight original books were rebuilt
to match. The seven short editions could not be: they had been set by another desk, in LibreOffice
at 6 × 9, and the note of that day said their front matter — the Artist's Note, How to read this
book, The words — had never been in the library's text, so a rebuild from that text would drop it.

**That was wrong, and the test says so.** `WC/tools/short_coverage_0925.py` extracts every sentence
of each served PDF and asks which are not in the source. Nothing came back but the line breaks the
extraction itself makes, and the sentences the corrections had already replaced. The title page,
the Artist's Note, How to read this book, The words, The Axiom, every chapter, the epilogue and the
colophon all stand in `<library>/source/<book>/00_front.txt`, `ch*.txt` and `99_epilogue.txt` — the
same text the pages are built from, and therefore the corrected text.

So the seven were set here, from that text, and nothing in them was typed by hand
(`WC/tools/short_editions_0925.py`): 6 × 9.02 inches, the measure and the sizes of the edition each
replaces — body 11.5pt on 14.4, chapter 18pt, section 14pt — and the house face, **Atkinson
Hyperlegible**, which the Five Doors editions already used and which the Ø Models editions could
not, that desk having had DejaVu Sans. Exported through Word, as every book of record here is.

The four Ø Models editions print their version on the colophon page, and it is in the library's own
text, so it moved with them. The three Five Doors editions print none — their version is the label
their book page carries, and that moved on the page.

Forty-nine corrections of 24 September and the word *corpus* — fifty-two checks that had never
passed — are in the download a reader takes, and `verify_downloads_0924.py` now reads **89 checks,
0 failures**. The dated note on the seven book pages, which said the download was behind its pages,
comes off with them (`stale_pdf_note_0924.py --off`).

**The Interior is 2.0.7 · 25 September 2026** (`WC/tools/interior_207_0925.py`), built from a copy
of 2.0.6's own source, hashed before and after and never opened for writing. One entry moved: the
open-debts chapter now says D-ID.1 is **closed** on the author's ruling of 24 September 2026 — the
floor is performed, as the axiom is, and what is performed is not formalised before it stands — and
keeps, in the same breath, what the book said until that day. 270 pages, unchanged. The version
line stands on the last page only, as the author asked. 2.0.6 is lodged here.

Every PDF in this folder is a file that stood on the wall until the date in its name. None is
edited; they are kept, as the Artist's Proofs keep theirs.
