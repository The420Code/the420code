Ø Instruments

The source of record beside every Artist’s Proof: the file its published PDF was built from, checked paragraph by paragraph against that PDF. Where no file matches, the folder says so, and the published PDF is its own source.

The check runs in both directions. Every paragraph of sixty characters or more in the source must be in the published PDF, and every sentence of sixty characters or more in the PDF must be in the source.

The folders also keep the earlier PDFs a republish replaced, and the note that says why. Frozen files are never edited: a correction is a dated note beside the locked body, never a change inside it.
