## Why these notes are beside the paper and not inside it

No source of record exists for AP10: the closest file differs from the published text, as
`SOURCE_OF_RECORD.md` in this folder shows paragraph by paragraph. Rebuilding the body from the
published PDF would mean re-typesetting a locked paper, and an error introduced that way would be
invisible in a few years' time. So these notes stand beside the published PDF, which is
untouched: its digest holds, and every record that cites it still resolves. When a matching
source is produced, the paper is republished with these notes appended after its body, as AP28's
were on 7 September 2026.
