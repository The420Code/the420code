0.1 — What this paper does

AP01 installs the four formal Papers through which The 420 Code does its operational work. Each Paper defines an operational quantity, proves the theorems that quantity obeys, and ties each load-bearing claim to at least one published falsifier with a specified observable.

Four claims, each derived.

First. There is an operational measure of record-structured irreversibility — the Actualization State (AS) — definable on any system that admits a physically realizable coarse-graining, monotone under decohering dynamics, and bounded between zero and one. Paper A produces the definition, the monotonicity theorem (T1), and the no-return-surface result (T2).

Second. Selection between alternatives — the transition from multiplicity to definiteness — is a costly, rate-limited, irreversible exclusion process. The selection cost is non-zero, the selection rate is bounded above by ΔE_G/ℏ for gravitationally distinguishable records (under the gravity-limited hypothesis), and selection cannot precede operational irreversibility. Paper B produces the definitions, the structural requirements on the selection channel, and the rate inequality with its kill switches.

Third. Agency is the operator-relative measure of the viability kernel reachable from a current state under admissible control. Agency decays under irreversible drift unless control compensates; the viability kernel has no-return surfaces that act as absorbing boundaries. Paper C produces the geometric definition, the control-budget and fatigue results, the slack and redundancy structure, and the exit-as-control-outcome treatment.

Fourth. Joint agency under coupling is non-additive. Stable configurations under drift satisfy specific structural conditions — impedance matching, anti-resonance avoidance, non-cascade propagation. Emergent order — hierarchy, cooperation, deterrence — falls out of the geometry, not from imported preferences. Paper D produces the joint-agency formalism, the structural-filtering result, and the falsifiers for each derived ordering.

The paper makes one further methodological claim. The four quantities are defined operationally — they can be measured — and tied to twenty-four falsifiers that specify the observable conditions under which each load-bearing claim would fail. KS-V.1 (operational invariance of AS itself) is the global kill switch. If it fires, the entire structure fails.

0.3 — Axiom mapping

Axiom R is principal load-bearing across Papers A, B, C, and D. Records do not unwrite. Operational irreversibility is the structural fact R produces in the presence of finite C. Without R, the Actualization State has nothing to measure, no-return surfaces collapse, and selection has no irreversibility to be costly relative to.

Axiom B is load-bearing for Paper B. The break — selection between alternatives — is what the costly exclusion process accomplishes. Without B, multiplicity does not resolve to definiteness, and the operational distinction between the pre-measurement ensemble and the post-measurement individual realization does not exist.

Axiom C is load-bearing for Papers A, C, and D. The bounded propagation through which records reach is what produces the operator horizon (T2), the no-return surfaces in viability theory, and the finite viability kernels on which agency is defined. Without C, no-return surfaces don’t exist and agency collapses to a uniform unbounded predicate.

Axiom S provides the structure of the substrate within which records are written and distinguishable sectors exist. Without S, the physically realizable coarse-graining 𝓞 of Paper A has nothing to range over, and the record algebra has no distinct sectors to be selected between.

0.6 — Structural debts owed

None outstanding from this paper. AP01 installs operational apparatus and its falsifiers; its load-bearing claims are derived within the paper or are framed as postulates with explicit kill switches.

The gravity-limited rate hypothesis (postulate G in Paper A, B4 in Paper B, with falsifiers G1–G3 / F_G1–F_G3) is a falsifiable postulate carrying its own kill switches, not a structural debt. If it fails, Papers A and B retreat to weaker claims about selection rate; Paper C’s agency apparatus and Paper D’s coupling apparatus are unaffected.

The objective-actualisation-channel postulate (postulate P in A4.2) is a falsifiable postulate, not a structural debt. Its kill switches are KS-V.2 through KS-V.4 (pointer-basis targeting, Born statistics, context independence). If it fires, the work retreats to decoherence-without-actualisation, and Papers A and B’s irreversibility results survive at the ensemble level.

0.7 — Items held open without claiming debt status

Three items are bracketed inside this paper. They are real and live, but they are not structural debts — they are operational specifications, parameter identifications, and cross-AP integration work.

The relativistic extension of Paper A’s quantum-mechanical instantiation. Section A4 is non-relativistic; the gravitational rate bound is treated in the weak-field limit. The relativistic form is open work. This is operational specification, not structural derivation.

The first-principles derivation of the coupling parameters in Papers C and D — the control budget B₀, the cost function c(u), the impedance Z_i, the resonance phase. These are parameterised in the structural account so the theorems can be stated and the falsifiers specified; their physical instantiations in specific embodied systems are open work for domain experts. This is parameter identification, not structural derivation.

The relationship between AP01’s records-side apparatus and AP43’s pre-state-side apparatus. AP01 installs the operational measure of record-structured irreversibility — the body has already written records, and AS measures how irreversibly. AP43 installs the operator’s interface with the pre-state through coupling capacity, pattern, pattern coherence, tracking coherence — the body reads the slope of what could be written next. The two are duals across the now: AP08 (gravity from records on the manifold) and AP43 (gravity of possibilities on the pre-state) inherit this dual structure. The formal integration of AS-on-records with AP43’s amplitude-landscape apparatus is open work for the relativistic field-equation paper (closing AP43’s D1).
