Claim Summary

section 0 (Dependency and Scope). Contract with the reader. Dependencies named (axiom as primitive; AP40 as the prior chapter in Notebook I; AP43 as structural dual; the Ø Models companions). Axiom mapping across S, B, R, C. Epistemic status per section. Twenty-four kill switches summarised. No outstanding structural debts. Three items held open without claiming debt status. Structural relationships to AP07, AP08, AP25, AP43, and the Ø Models companions named.

Paper 0 (Foundations). NARRATIVE. The structural story that motivated the work — nothing becomes structure; structure once written cannot be unwritten; the loop runs at the actualising now. Explicitly non-load-bearing. The four formal Papers stand or fall on their own logic, independent of the narrative.

Paper A (Actualization State: An Operational Measure of Record-Structured Irreversibility). DEFINITION + DERIVATION. Five core definitions (D1–D5). Theorem T1 (monotonicity of AS under decohering dynamics) with rate corollary T1a and converse T1b. Theorem T2 (operator horizon — no-return as an inequality). No-return surfaces and operational irreversibility (D6–D10, P3.3). Quantum-mechanical instantiation (D11–D16, postulate P, postulate G, P4.1). Seven falsifiers: F0 (global, KS-V.1), F1–F3 (selection postulate, KS-V.2–V.4), G1–G3 (gravitational, KS-V.5–V.7). Optional turn module (A6) and appendices.

Paper B (Selection as Irreversible Exclusion). STRUCTURAL DERIVATION. Definiteness reframed as operational individual-realization status. Nonlinearity requirement and selection cost made operational (δ_sel formalised in B2.3, B2.4). Six structural requirements on selection dynamics (B3.1–B3.6). Universal rate constraints with gravity as candidate limiter (B4). Five experimental regimes (BT1–BT5). Kill switch B2 (KS-V.8) on pre-irreversibility selection. Programmatic closure: no further progress on selection can be made by argument alone; the remaining uncertainty is empirical.

Paper C (Agency as Constrained Control). DEFINITION + DERIVATION. Agency as operator-relative ratio of reachable-within-viability to viability (C1). Control authority approaching zero at no-return surfaces. Drift under irreversibility (C2). Necessary conditions for agency preservation (C3). No-return geometry within a realised sector (C4). Control budgets and fatigue (C5 with theorem C5.1). Noise and silence (C6). Coupling and rescue (C7). Slack and robustness (C8). Exit as control outcome (C9). Falsifiers FC1–FC5 (KS-V.9–V.13). Structural closure with experimental instantiation (bacterial chemotaxis, autonomous robotic navigation).

Paper D (Coupled Viability). DEFINITION + DERIVATION. Shared constraint environments with Geometric Exclusion Principle (D1.3). Composition of agency: non-additivity, impedance matching, anti-resonance (D2). Stable configurations under drift with necessary (D3.2a) and sufficient (D3.2b) conditions; cascade failure (D3.3). Emergent order without design: structural filtering (D4.2), hierarchy (D4.3), cooperation and deterrence as geometric outcomes (D4.4). Eleven falsifiers FD0–FD4.4b (KS-V.14–V.24). Structural closure with worked examples and Appendix E flagged as exploratory and non-load-bearing.
