Claim Summary

section 0 (Dependency and Scope). Contract with the reader. Dependencies named (axiom as primitive, AP40, AP01, AP09, AP16 section 5, the Ø Models companions). Axiom mapping across S, B, R, C with each axiom forced by its conceptual precondition. Epistemic status per section. Six kill switches summarised (KS-7, KS-P.1, KS-P.2, KS-P.3, KS-P.4, KS-P.5). No outstanding structural debts. Two items held open without claiming debt status.

section 1 (What EH Says). FRAMING. The Embedding Hypothesis stated and the conditional under which every result from AP05 through AP19 and AP24 has been read until now. The common misreading (algebraic vs geometric as two claims) named and deferred to section 5.

section 2 (What EH Failing Would Mean). STRUCTURAL ARGUMENT. Reality has no alternative structure to {S, B, R, C} that produces records. The pre-state (1:1) is the subject of the axioms, not a competitor. There is no third option: either the 1:1 is unbroken (no records, no observation) or records exist (axioms satisfied).

section 3 (The Undeniable Premise). DERIVATION. At least one record exists — the denial is itself a record. The forcing argument: distinction forces S, difference forces B, persistence forces R, locality forces C. Zero gap between concept and axiom. The axioms ARE the concepts.

section 4 (Completeness and Minimality). STATEMENT AND PROOF SKETCH. Completeness and minimality stated, proof-sketched, and fenced at KS-P.1 and KS-P.2 as the open formalisation obligations. Section 4.2a states the two results and their proof obligations. What completeness and minimality mean for EH: no fifth axiom is needed; none of the four is derivable from the others.

section 5 (The Actualization State and the Manifold). STRUCTURAL IDENTITY. The Actualization State IS the manifold. The apparent gap between algebra and geometry is shown to be the same claim read from two sides. The eye cannot see its own retina — there is no platform outside the manifold from which to observe the embedding. EH is one claim, not two. QRA collapses by the same identity.

section 6 (The Proof). FORMAL CLOSURE. Five-step modus ponens. Premise: at least one record exists. Forcing: records require {S, B, R, C}. Completeness: no fifth axiom. Minimality: no axiom derivable from others. Identity: AS IS the manifold. Conclusion: reality satisfies {S, B, R, C}, therefore EH.

section 7 (Self-Proving, Not Circular). STRUCTURAL ARGUMENT. The proof is reflexive: the act of questioning EH is itself a record, and a record requires the axioms. Not circular — the premise is undeniable rather than assumed. The measurement constraint: any test of the axioms is performed from within the axioms.

section 8 (The Two Cases). STRUCTURAL ARGUMENT. Exactly two cases. Case 1: no records exist (1:1 unbroken, question cannot be asked, no asker). Case 2: at least one record exists (EH holds). There is no Case 3 — no reality that contains records but does not satisfy the conditions for records.

section 9 (Consequences). ENUMERATION. AP05 through AP19 and AP24 become unconditional. Problem 7 (EH as theorem) is closed. QRA is closed by the same argument that closes EH. No bridge hypotheses remain in the work.

section 10 (Kill Switches). FORMAL FALSIFICATION REGISTER. KS-7 (EH, CLOSED). KS-P.1 (completeness of the axiom set, LIVE — HARD). KS-P.2 (minimality of the axiom set, LIVE — HARD). KS-P.3 (record definition / forcing argument, LIVE — HARD). KS-P.4 (QRA, CLOSED). KS-P.5 (smoothness of the Actualization State, LIVE — EMPIRICAL). Each in Claim · Test · Status · Recovery form.

section 11 (What This Establishes and What Remains Open). SYNTHESIS. EH closed as theorem. Four structural attack points named (KS-P.1, KS-P.2, KS-P.3, KS-P.5). Two items held open without claiming debt status. Philosophical-register companions named: Dissolutions Chapter 1 (Why Is There Something Rather Than Nothing?), Resolutions Chapter 2 (The Source of Mathematics), Resolutions Chapter 4 (Laws of Nature). Placement in The 420 Code: Chapter 3 of Notebook I — The Premise. Notebook I structurally complete with AP40, AP01, AP20 locked.
