Artist’s Note

This is Artist’s Proof 02 of The 420 Code, a chapter of Notebook VII — The Operator Interface: Consciousness, Ethic, Reading. It derives the human-scale physics of agency. An operator is any sovereign agent that applies control input to hold its structure against entropic drift — a person, a bacterium, a corporation, a civilisation. The definition requires no consciousness; it requires a budget, a drift, a corridor, and an exit.

Nine theorems follow from six standard physical axioms — energy is conserved, time is irreversible, substrates are finite, structure decays without maintenance, observation is noisy, and some failure states are absorbing. Every claim is formalised, every result is falsifiable, and no claim depends on intention, narrative, identity, or belief.

The paper’s terminal result is structural, not moral. Coupling between self-maintaining agents is sustainable only when each can pay its own drift; mutual non-extraction between such agents is the control-theoretic content of kindness. Don’t be a cunt. Be kind — derived, not commanded; forced by the structure together with the one-interior identity (AP43 §9).

Nothing here is imported. The six axioms are projections of the body of work axioms {S, B, R, C} onto the human scale; AP02 can be read on its own footing or as the 420 Code’s physics read at the scale of a body.

Orientation

Where this paper fits in the body of work

The 420 Code is organised across eight bound notebooks. Each notebook is a standalone book carrying one division of the architecture; the eight together carry the full body of work. AP02 is a chapter of Notebook VII — The Operator Interface. AP43, the Notebook’s opener, installs the four-term system through which an operator reads the amplitude landscape: coupling capacity, pattern, pattern coherence, tracking coherence. AP02 derives what that operator does under constraint — how it maintains structure, where it fails, when it must withdraw. AP01 (The Actualization State) provides the lineage: AP02 is recognisably AP01’s programme projected onto the human-scale control problem. The dependency is structural, not formal — no result in AP02 requires another Artist’s Proof to hold.

How to read this paper

The reading order is front to back. Section 0 names the dependencies and the scope. The Preface states the six axioms you already live under. Derivations I–V specify the autonomous operator — law, solution, limit, method, filter. Derivations VI–VIII specify coupled operators — link, cascade, firewall. Derivation IX specifies exit. The Closure draws the ethical consequence. Two registers alternate: the formal derivations carry the mathematics a control theorist can check, and the prose around them says, in plain language, what each result means for a body.

What this paper does and does not do

AP02 derives nine theorems and eleven formal results about agency, conduct, and exit, and grounds the terminal ethic in the coupling geometry. It establishes the two-agent coupling tax (Theorem VI.1) and, at network scale, the conditional containment theorem (Theorem VI.2, DERIVED-CONDITIONAL) that partially closes the one declared debt (Debt D1): the persistent set is confined to the net-positive class. The quantitative matching criterion D1 names remains owed, and Theorem VI.2 — conditional on operator properties — is distinct in class from the eleven results forced from the six axioms. It does not depend on consciousness, and it makes no claim outside thermodynamics, information theory, control engineering, and network dynamics.

The kill switches

KS-O.1 — Drift-discipline duality

Claim. In any system with entropic drift, holding non-zero structure requires continuous, non-zero control input. Test. Exhibit a system with entropic drift that maintains its structure indefinitely under zero control input (Appendix C, D1).

KS-O.2 — Capacity horizon

Claim. Every embodied system has a maximum control capacity u_max; once required maintenance ax exceeds u_max, structure decays even at maximum effort, and collapse is deterministic. Test. Exhibit a system that reliably maintains or grows structure while required maintenance persistently exceeds its capacity (Appendix C, D3).

KS-O.3 — Exit optimality

Claim. When a system’s structural dynamics guarantee net loss over any sufficient horizon, withdrawal dominates every admissible strategy. Test. Exhibit a structurally negative-sum system in which continued participation beats withdrawal over the long run (Appendix C, D9).

KS-COUP.1

If a net-negative configuration persists past its collapse bound, or undefended parasitism or pure defection persists against a defended counterparty, the containment theorem (Theorem VI.2) fails. LIVE — STRUCTURAL (conditional).

Every kill switch in this paper is filed in the registry, on the420code.org: the420code.org/killswitches/
