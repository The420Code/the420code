8.3 — Foliation independence [Layer 3]

The Einstein field equations describe a 4-dimensional manifold without a preferred global time function. The ‘bounce’ appears under specific foliations; the structural identity is atemporal. Mathematically permissible: no preferred global time function is required by the Einstein equations.

8.4 — The anisotropy problem [Layer 2]

Open Problem L-Aniso (KS-L0.8). The Schwarzschild interior is Kantowski-Sachs (anisotropic). Observation requires FRW (isotropic).
