Multiplied out with the measured α and mₑ and three ruled structural choices, one full cycle is (21/18)·α⁻¹⁸·τ_C = 13.830 billion years, against a measured age of 13.787. That chain is frozen here as the author’s claim, wearing its provenance — in the order it happened — and its coarse precision on its face.

Fourth. The stance, and D5. The settled leak’s fractional rate is a constant of structure — in the surveys’ language, its own equation of state is w = −1 — while what the surveys measure is AP42’s sourced reservoir and the sky’s unfinished settling. And the cosmological limb of Notebook VII’s D5 is paid here: AP21’s tension, AP42’s reservoir, and the leakage register are one hold read three ways.
