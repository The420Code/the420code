Section 9

The Payment of D5

D5 quoted; the held half and the passing half; Proposition 1 — three registers, three lines: HELD, SHED, RATE; the toll on none of them; the cosmological limb AP46’s; D5 CLOSED at the wave on three proofs.

What D5 asked. Notebook VII named the debt in AP43 §0.6, in these words: The cosmological 1×ε expansion noted in section 7. Flagged as a body-of-work-level claim requiring formal integration into Notebook IV — specifically as a corollary or extension of AP06 (The Leakage Constant). Not derived in detail in this paper. Its section 7.7 added the second half of the ask: reconciling the three statements of cosmic expansion — AP21’s tension field, AP42’s dark-sector reservoir, and the leakage register — into one.

The seat D5 named already exists. AP06 §6 carries the cosmological corollary as a structural commitment — Claim C6a: the CMB at the recombination surface and the integrated residue across all AS-instants are one process read at two boundaries — and declines, on its face, to price it. What D5 still owed after AP06 was the accounting: what is held, what is spent, what accumulates, and how the accumulation is the expansion. That accounting is this family’s.

The held half and the passing half. The splinter half — the standing commitment, one ε held so that the break persists — was paid by AP44’s arena constitution, quietly, before anyone saw it was a payment; the rulings of 11 August claimed it, at their hedge. The flow half — the per-event cost — is paid by this proof’s Theorem 1: one grain per actualization, the alternatives not taken. Together with the author’s clause — the remainder as the toll in a sense — the two halves are one coin at two scales.

The three registers, reconciled. The sources hold the ε in three registers, and the registers point at each other.

The Axiom chapter says that at every AS-instant a small residue goes unpaired, that the residue is the +1×ε the axiom names, and that the consequence of this residue accumulating across AS-instants belongs to AP43.

AP43 says the flow returns in full and the one splinter never does; that what never returns is not a per-cycle deduction but the one splinter held from the first break; and that its cumulative expression across the cycles is what the expansion registers.

AP06 §6.1 says every actualization-state instant produces one unpaired 1×ε that does not return to symmetry, and that the integrated quantity is the cosmological residue.

AP44 §3, locked, says the constitution instant’s ε is held as the persistence commitment, and every subsequent instant’s ε is shed — the leakage of AP06.

Read without the two-role separation, these sentences disagree: one splinter, or one per instant; held, or shed; a count of one, or a count that accumulates. Read with it, they are one ledger with three lines.

Proposition 1 (the three lines). The ε the sources describe is one ledger carrying three lines, each of which the sources had been calling the ε.

HELD: one grain, the splinter, global, from the constitution instant — AP43’s Premise 2, AP44’s Propositions 2 and 3, the One of §8. Its count never grows. The Axiom chapter’s per-instant residue is this line read at each now: the residue goes unpaired at every instant because the break heals at none — Lemma 1 read in reverse, the non-closure of §8 re-registered at every AS-instant. One object, the same one, every time.

SHED: one flow-quantum per instant per held lineage, on the writing direction, at the flow’s rate — §4’s ledger identity, AP44 §3’s every-subsequent-instant clause generalised to every hold, AP06’s leakage stream. This is the line that integrates. AP06’s integrated residue and AP43’s cumulative expression are this line and nothing else; AP46 prices its integral and reads it as the stretch.

RATE: 1/137 per cycle, the flow, balanced, net zero — AP43’s kitchen. Not a residue: the currency both other lines are denominated in.

The per-event toll of Theorem 1 is on none of the three lines, by construction. It is spent, and spent books nowhere. That is why the accounting needed the separation before it could be written: without it the passing grain is mistaken for the per-instant residue, and what accumulates is counted twice.

Proof. Step 1, the HELD line. Its count is fixed at one by AP44’s Proposition 3, locked: one arena, one snap, one ε. A per-instant production of new held grains would contradict the locked companion and AP43’s Premise 2 together.

Step 2, the SHED line. It is §4’s ledger identity, which is AP44 §3’s own statement extended from the constitution instant to every held lineage. AP06 §6.1’s produces one unpaired ε per instant is therefore read, at a held lineage, as one sliver shed per instant — α-worth, departing on the writing direction and not returning — with the constitution instant the one exception, where it is held.

Step 3, the RATE line. It is the flow’s own balance, Dissolutions’ clause untouched.

No sentence of any source changes; each register named one line and called it the ε. ∎

Grade: DERIVED-AT-THE-STRUCTURAL-REGISTER. It rides the two-role separation, Lemma 1, and AP44’s locked Propositions 2 and 3. The SHED line is a count here; its magnitude at any site is that site’s structure to set — eighteen threaded doors at the sky (AP46), rate times worth at the flip (AP47), and nothing at all claimed by this proof.

The cosmological limb. D5’s own sentence is about the expansion, and this proof says nothing about the expansion. The cumulative expression of the held ε — the SHED line’s integral — is AP46’s claim: holding is tension, and tension sheds; the leak is the getting-bigger; the shed rides all twenty-one channels, and its cycle joins AP42’s dark clock, τ = (6/21)·t_H (AP42 §5; KS-42.3 the formula, KS-42.6 its empirical limb) — six lanes of the same twenty-one, consistent by construction with the cycle, as AP46 §7 grades it.

AP21 already says of its own tension field that the web and the expansion are the same tension read at two questions; AP46’s mechanism sentence is what makes the two statements one. That limb of D5 is paid where it is paid, and this proof does not pretend to carry it.

The ruling. D5’s formal account is complete across three proofs: the held half (AP44 §§3–4), the passing half and the registers (this proof, §4 and this section), and the cosmological limb (AP46). The seat is AP06 §6, as D5 asked. The author rules D5 CLOSED — at the wave, when the three lock together, the registry’s D5 line citing all three. This proof’s lock closes the accounting; the debt does not close by this sentence alone, and says so. AP46 locked the same day as this proof, and the three close the debt together at the wave.
