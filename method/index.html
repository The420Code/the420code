Logic first. Every step forced. The physics fell out.

The physics is the same logic carried on until it meets measurement, with one measured number: α, the fine-structure constant. The physics is why the ethic carries weight.

It is not numerology. Numerology starts from a measured number and hunts for a formula that hits it. This work starts from the axiom, and every step is forced. The count reaches one integer, twenty-one, before physics has entered the conversation. From the quantum to the cosmic, that one integer, unchanged, carries three unrelated measurements. The work searched every formula of the same size: 2,971 reach the proton at least as closely as its first-order formula, and each is a fixed number, with nothing to carry to a second measurement.

The test numerology never runs on itself

A numerologist searches. So the work ran the search — against itself. Every formula of the same size, built from the integers 1 to 30, π and arithmetic, was aimed at the proton ratio. 2,971 reach it at least as closely as the work’s first-order formula, and fifteen reach the measurement’s own eleven-digit bar at order α. That is what a search can do: it can always hit one number. That is why one number, however many digits, is never the claim.

None of those 2,971 has a second life. Each is a fixed number, with no integer in it to carry to gravity or to the sky. The twenty-one carries three, from the quantum to the cosmic. That is the claim, and nothing in the search matches it.

When the logic broke, the logic was fixed

A kill switch does not test a number. It tests the logic from that point downstream. On 2 August 2026 the bare neutron–proton row fired at 7.24σ. The work published it the same day, and it stays on the wall exactly as it was.

That is one principle — holding costs — in three forms: at the neutron, a held distinction priced at order α² over 8π (AP47); at the arena, one held ε, a factor of 1/(1 + α) (AP44); at the sky, the rate closed from the partition and the cycle (AP46, AP48).
