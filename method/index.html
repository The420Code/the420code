The Method

Logic first. Every step forced. The physics fell out.

The 420 Code is a work of ethics. Its claim is that “be kind” is derived — from one axiom, one record exists, with no measured input at all. The physics is the same logic carried on until it meets measurement, with one measured number: α, the fine-structure constant. The physics is why the ethic carries weight.

It is not numerology. Numerology starts from a measured number and hunts for a formula that hits it. This work starts from the axiom, and every step is forced. The count reaches one integer, twenty-one, before physics has entered the conversation. From the quantum to the cosmic, that one integer, unchanged, carries three unrelated measurements. The work searched every formula of the same size: 2,971 reach the proton alone, and not one reaches a second measurement.

The floor — forced, step by step

  1. One record exists. Try to deny it — the denial is a record. These words are the proof. AP20 The Proof
  2. Four conditions. A record needs a symmetry to break, a break, a record that holds, and a constraint so it can be read: Symmetry, Break, Record, Constraint. Remove any one and no record can be written or read. AP01 · AP05
  3. Six readings. A record is held and read in six ways — six readings of one grain: mass, gravity, light, resistance, grip, time. Six, and the registry carries the switch that fires on more or fewer (KS-D.1). AP24 The Residual
  4. Three dimensions. Four conditions give four directions a record can differ in. One is the direction the record flows from now — time. Three remain. That is space. AP10 The Dimension
  5. Read now. Every record is written and read now — every attempt to deny now happens now. The now couples once to each of the three dimensions. AP28 · AP50

6 × 3 + 3 = 21

Six readings, each held in three directions: eighteen. The now, once in each direction: three. Twenty-one. Not twenty-two — time is not a direction the now couples to. It is the record of the hold.

There is no choice anywhere in that chain. Remove a condition and no record can be read. Change the readings and the grain is not the grain. Change the dimensions and they no longer come from the four conditions. Up to here, physics has not entered the conversation. No mass has been weighed. No constant has been looked up. The twenty-one is a count.

“I have one thing in hand — one record exists. Deny it, and you prove it. A record needs four conditions. Conditions are read and records are held in six ways — the six constants — over three dimensions, and every dimension is held and read now. That is twenty-one. Nothing was argued. Every step is forced — there is no room to manoeuvre. Up to this point, physics has not even entered the conversation. From here we built, and from here we touched physics — from our own floor. Measurements were never the target. There never was a target. The job was, and always will be, to develop the logic of one record exists. The physics fell out of the logic.”

— G

Then the physics, from the floor

From here the same twenty-one is carried outward, and it meets measurement three times — from the inside of a proton to the whole of the universe.

The proton. The twenty-one against itself, once in each of the four directions; against the three couplings of now; and the three against each other: 21² × 4 + 21 × 3 + 3² = 1836. A proton weighs 1836.15 electrons. The last digits are leakage — the cost of the flow — from the same twenty-one. AP30 · AP49

The test numerology never runs on itself

A numerologist searches. So the work ran the search — against itself. Every formula of the same size, built from the integers 1 to 30, π and arithmetic, was aimed at the proton ratio. 2,971 reach it as well as the work’s first-order formula does, and fifteen reach it closer. That is what a search can do: it can always hit one number. That is why one number, however many digits, is never the claim.

Not one of those 2,971 formulas reaches a second measurement. They hit one number and stop. The twenty-one hits three, from the quantum to the cosmic. That is the claim, and nothing in the search matches it.

The search covered one coefficient, not three formulas at once, so a rival built some other way is not ruled out — and the switch that would record it, KS-30.4, stays live. The challenge is open to anyone: build a rival for three, out of one integer. verify_between_family.py · the search, 4 September 2026

When the logic broke, the logic was fixed

A kill switch does not test a number. It tests the logic from that point downstream. On 2 August 2026 the bare neutron–proton row fired at 7.24σ. The work published it the same day, and it stays on the wall exactly as it was.

A fired switch says where a link in the chain broke. This link had priced a held distinction as free — and holding costs. Priced, the neutron landed at −0.005σ (AP47). The same correction, carried downstream, moved the gravitational constant from +0.69% to −0.036% (AP44), and the same mechanism at the scale of the universe replaced a fired expansion rate, 74.3, with 67.45, at 0.1σ (AP46, AP48). One repair, made in the logic, and three fields moved toward measurement.

A fit cannot do that. A fit has no way to produce its own counter-evidence, and no way for one repair to improve three fields at once.

Where the logic caught up

Where a number arrived before the logic reached it, the record says so, dated and in public. The errata and the registry carry these:

  • The sixteen. The proton’s second-order term carries 16. Its value was pinned by the measured ratio — 336 = 21 × 16 — before its structure was written. AP49 The Hold then derived it: four conditions of a record, held in three dimensions over time — four times four (6 September 2026).
  • The commitment in gravity. The value ε = α in gravity’s (1 + α) was first selected among structural candidates (AP44), then read by rule at AP50; the debt closed on 11 September 2026. KS-CCC.1 fires on any choice behind G made by comparison with the measured value and left undisclosed. It has not fired.
  • The proton’s fourth condition. On 2 August 2026 a second way of writing 1836 in the same terms surfaced. A fourth condition leaves one, and the errata say it was stated after the alternative appeared.

That is the method in the open: logic first, measurement as the check, and every catch on the record beside the switch that would fire.

Evidence now, proof on dates

Nine numbers are on the wall, from the quantum to the cosmic, from one measured input. Seven land inside one standard deviation of the measurement they face. That is evidence. It is not yet final proof — final proof comes from measurements that have not been made, and every frozen prediction names the one that would kill it: Ø Frozen Predictions.

Most of those measurements were known before the logic reached them — as Mercury’s orbit was known for fifty-six years before Einstein derived it. When a measurement was taken says nothing about how a number was reached. How it was reached is above: count first, then compute.

The next measurements will say which way.
