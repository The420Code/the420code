# Glyph sweep — 7 September 2026

**Ruling of 7 September 2026:** no decorative glyph anywhere, ever — U+2728 and every character in U+2700–27BF, U+2600–26FF, U+1F000–1FAFF. Mathematical and scientific symbols are untouched.

**This sweep modified nothing.** Removal is dress, not body: it happens at the file's next republish or rebind and is recorded in that republish note by codepoint.

**Scanned:** 166 text files and 190 PDFs in the site repository, and 109 docx sources under `00 - KDP`. `museum/` is MC's and is not swept here.

## 317 raw hits — of which **192 are decorative**

The raw count is misleading on its own, so it is sorted first. Two of the five characters found live inside the ruling's ranges but are not ornament.

| Codepoint | Count | Class | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `U+2726` | 136 | **DECORATIVE** | a four-pointed star used as a section divider (— [U+2726] —) |
| `U+2713` | 116 | **FUNCTIONAL** | a tick marking a verification that passed, in the browser verifier's own output — content, not ornament |
| `U+2728` | 50 | **DECORATIVE** | the sparkle; the character the ruling names first |
| `U+2609` | 9 | **SCIENTIFIC** | the astronomical symbol for the Sun, in solar masses (10⁸ M[U+2609]) — exempt by the ruling's own terms |
| `U+26A0` | 6 | **DECORATIVE** | a warning sign used for emphasis in an internal working file |

**For G's ruling.** `U+2609` is exempt on the ruling's own words — it is the astronomical symbol for the Sun and appears only as solar masses in an astrophysical bound. `U+2713` is the tick the browser verifier prints when a derivation passes; it is the output's content, not its dress, and removing it would take meaning out of the page. The desk should confirm both, and if either is ruled decorative the sweep is re-run with it reclassified.
