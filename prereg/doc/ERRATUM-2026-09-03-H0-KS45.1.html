2. The erratum on the width

AP18 §5 tests the floor a₀ = C_S² · cH₀/(2π) against an empirical acceleration scale quoted as 1.20 ± 0.02 × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s², citing McGaugh et al. 2016 and Lelli et al. 2017, and §§7–8 invert the relation to H₀ = 74.3 ± 1.2.

The ±0.02 is the random error alone. The sources cited give the scale as 1.20 ± 0.02 (random) ± 0.24 (systematic) × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s², the systematic arising from the stellar mass-to-light ratios and the distances. Propagated, the floor's inversion is H₀ = 74.3 ± 14.9, not ± 1.2. The relation cannot distinguish 67 from 74. The registration of 2026-08-02 said as much in its own §6 ("the ± 1.2 in the registered value is the optimistic branch") and registered the sharp width anyway; the sharp width is what fired.

What this changes, in order:

  1. KS-45 stands, live. The coefficient a₀/(cH₀) = 0.1662 sits at 0.46σ against the sky's 0.183 ± 0.037 at H₀ = 67.45.
  2. The floor survives as a relation. At 67.45 it gives 1.089 × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s², 9.2% below the scale and 0.46σ on the honest error; across the corpus's window it stays between 0.24σ and 0.67σ.
  3. The symbol. AP18 writes its geometric correction factor 2 ln(sec ½ + tan ½) ≈ 1.0445 as α; the symbol collides with the fine-structure constant. The site, the registry and the glossary write it C_S²; the papers follow at their next revision.
  4. Nothing in AP18's derivation of the form or the coefficient moves. What moves is the width of one number and the name of one symbol.
