Pre-registration: α(0) does not vary across the record history

Freeze date: 2026-09-11 Author: G · Studio G · the420code.org Scope: AP51 (The Glass), FINAL v1.0, §4 and §10; carried by KS-GLASS.3. Status: frozen in the wave of the four, at the author’s word. The commit hash is the timestamp: c9c02cd, tagged wave-2026-09-11.

1. The claim

The fine-structure constant at zero momentum transfer, α(0), compared under equivalent conditions, has no intrinsic variation across the record history — not with place, not with epoch, not with the density of the landscape. Every measurement of Δα/α returns a value consistent with zero within its uncertainty; individual estimates scatter, and the prediction is that no variation is confirmed at any precision.

2. Scope

Spatial and temporal variation of α(0). Not the running of the effective electromagnetic coupling with momentum transfer, which is AP50 §10’s question and D-EM.1’s.

3. Predicted

Δα/α = 0, structurally. No number of the corpus’s own. There is nothing here to fit and nothing to tune: the prediction is the absence of an effect.

4. Measured

Oklo; quasar absorption lines; atomic-clock comparisons. Contested detections exist — the spatial dipole claimed from quasar absorption in 2011 — attributed to systematics; the newest measurements return zero.

5. The kill condition

A variation of α(0) reported at five standard deviations or better, by two independent methods, standing after their systematics are reviewed. Then P-GLASS.1 is dead and shown; KS-GLASS.3 fires; AP51 §4’s universality is withdrawn; AP50 §3’s sorting stands or falls on its own.

6. What a null does not do

It does not single the hourglass out: every constant-α picture shares the prediction. What the hourglass adds is the reason — the grain is the same at every site and every tick, and the constraints are its readings. A null confirms nothing on its own. It is registered because a prediction that cannot distinguish must still be written down before the measurement, or the record is incomplete.
