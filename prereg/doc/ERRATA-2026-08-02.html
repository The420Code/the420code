E1 — α mislabelled (site, README, root verify.py)

1/137.035999084 is CODATA 2018. CODATA 2022 is 1/137.035999177(21). The corpus compares against CODATA 2022 measured values, so the inputs were mixed vintages. Effect on the proton prediction: 0.013 ppt — numerically irrelevant, the label was wrong.

E2 — uniqueness of the 1836 decomposition (Ø Predictions Part I, AP30 §3)

Ø Predictions states three conditions and reports exactly one survivor. Independent enumeration reproduces the eleven under the first two conditions and returns two under all three:

  • 21²×4 + 21×3 + 3² = 1764 + 63 + 9
  • 21²×3 + 21×3² + 3⁴×4 = 1323 + 189 + 324

A fourth condition — factor count per term, with multiplicity, non-increasing across the ordered triple — leaves exactly one. AP30's prose already states the criterion but as "fewer," which is strictly decreasing; the adopted decomposition is 3, 2, 2. AP30's wording is corrected to "no more than." Condition 4 was formalised 2026-08-02, after the alternative surfaced. Declared.

E6a — the neutron–proton difference. KS-NPP.1 has fired.

Predicted 2.53099393, measured 2.530988574 ± 0.00000074. The offset is 2.12 ppm against a measurement bar of 0.288 ppm — 7.24 σ, verified two independent ways (mass-ratio route and atomic-mass-unit route). The corpus reports "about two parts per million" without stating the measurement's own precision, which reads as agreement. Every occurrence must state the sigma. Record KS-NPP.1 as FIRED on its empirical limb; the structural limbs (KS-NPP.2 sign, KS-NPP.3 lattice) are unaffected. No repair is offered: the required correction is negative, and every existing term in both AP30 formulas is positive.

E6b — the visible fraction

The corpus quotes the observed value as 4.86 % and the deviation as 2.0 %. Recomputing from Planck 2018 gives 4.86–4.91 % depending on data column and normalisation; 4.86 is the bottom of that range. Against the midpoint the deviation is 2.5 %, i.e. 2.46 σ. State the range and name the convention.

E6c — the α² coefficient is now pinned (a strengthening, not a correction)

The measurement requires 0.1826623 ± 0.000601 — a 0.33 % constraint. The corpus value 21×16/1836 agrees at 0.57 σ, implying 16 = 15.97 ± 0.05. As a numerator over 1836 the requirement is 335.4 ± 1.1, and 336 is the only product of powers of {21, 3, 4} inside 3 σ. KS-30.3 is restated: the value is settled, the decomposition is what is owed.
