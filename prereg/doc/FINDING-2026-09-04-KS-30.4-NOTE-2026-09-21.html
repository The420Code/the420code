The arithmetic stands, both legs, exactly as the finding and the script print it.

Leg A. 2,971 formulas of five tokens or fewer, built from the integers 1 to 30, π and the four operations, reach the proton-to-electron ratio at least as accurately as the work’s first-order formula, and fifteen reach the measurement’s own eleven-digit bar at order α.

2. What is withdrawn

The finding’s sentence “Any presentation of this corpus that leads with the eleven digits is misleading, and should stop”, and the same sentence in the verdict the script prints, are withdrawn by the author.

The sentence judged a presentation, not a result, and it read as though the digits had been put forward to mislead. They were put forward as a result. A number reached by counting is not made misleading because a search can also reach it. That is a property of searches, which can always hit one number. It is not a property of the count. Leg A shows what one number cannot do on its own, for anyone. It says nothing about how this one was reached.

3. What replaces it

The digits are never the lead — not because leading with them misleads, but because they are not where the work starts.

The work starts from one record exists, and counts: four conditions, six readings, three dimensions, read now. 6 × 3 + 3 = 21, before any measured value enters. The claim is the count and what it carries: one integer, three unrelated measurements, three functional forms. That is the order the work was made in, and the order it is shown in — Ø The Method and Ø Why 21 Is Forced.
