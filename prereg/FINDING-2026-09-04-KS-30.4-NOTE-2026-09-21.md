## 2. What is withdrawn

The finding's sentence *"Any presentation of this corpus that leads with the eleven digits is misleading, and should stop"*, and the same sentence in the verdict the script prints, are withdrawn by the author.

A number reached by counting is not made misleading because a search can also reach it. That is a property of searches, which can always hit one number. It is not a property of the count. Leg A shows what one number cannot do on its own, for anyone. It says nothing about how this one was reached.
