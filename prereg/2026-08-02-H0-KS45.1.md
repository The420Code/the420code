**So the ± 1.2 in the registered value is the optimistic branch, and this
document says so.** The claim becomes sharp only when galaxy-dynamics
systematics tighten below about 5 %. That is a rotation-curve problem, not a
cosmology problem, and it is where the corpus should be pushing.

Read forwards, however, the test is already live: at the best current H₀ the
relation predicts a₀ = 1.187 × 10⁻¹⁰ and Planck's H₀ predicts 1.089 × 10⁻¹⁰.
