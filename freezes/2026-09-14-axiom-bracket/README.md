# Freeze — 14 September 2026, the α bracket

The **before-state** of every page and file changed by the ruling of 14 September 2026 (D98), taken
from the live site immediately before the change and digested.

`MANIFEST.json` carries, for each URL: its SHA-256, its byte length, whether it carried the old
bracket, and the file here that holds the served bytes.

The served copies are stored with a `.txt` suffix on purpose. They are evidence, not pages: a copy
kept at a `.html` path would be published by the deploy and would stand on the site as a second,
stale version of a page that has moved on. The bytes are unaltered — the digest in the manifest is
of exactly what is in the file beside it.

The old form of the axiom, `1:1 + 1×ε @ AS [+1/137 / −1/137]`, stays on the record as written. It is
what these copies show, and that is the point of them.
